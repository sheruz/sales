Questions

Before you get in touch.

Where is your team, and how do time zones work?

We work remotely with clients in North America, the UK, Europe and the Gulf. Every project gets agreed overlap hours for calls, plus async updates the rest of the day.

Who owns the code and designs?

You do. Code lives in your repository from the first week, and full ownership transfers with final payment.

How do payments work?

Fixed-price projects are split across milestones, so you pay as working features are delivered. Retainers are billed monthly in advance.

Can you take over an existing project?

Yes. We start with a short code audit, tell you plainly what state it is in, then propose a plan to stabilize and continue it.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes, before the discovery call if you prefer.

Can I use Delvix Autopilot for my business?

It is in early access for a small group of agencies and freelancers. Request access in the Autopilot section and we will reach out.
