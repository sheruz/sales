The problem

Finding clients took more time than doing the work.

Agencies and freelancers spend hours researching prospects, guessing who to contact and sending generic emails that get ignored. Follow-ups slip, and there is no single place to see what's working.

The approach

Lead with proof, not a pitch.

Instead of sending a generic offer, the platform opens each prospect's website in a real browser, records what's actually broken with screenshots, and writes a short email about that one real problem.
